# PDMPublisher Roadmap

This page describes planned work. See the product guides below for available features.

## Current Products

- [PDMPublisher for SOLIDWORKS](pdmpublishersolidworks.md) provides interactive publishing, document tools, BOM Manager, ERP Sync, and Publisher Macros inside SOLIDWORKS.
- [PDMPublisher PDM Task](pdmpublisher.md) provides automated and scheduled publishing through SOLIDWORKS PDM Professional.

## Planned Work

| Target | Product | Planned work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026–2027 | SOLIDWORKS add-in | Additional ERP Sync connectors for Microsoft Dynamics 365, SAP, Epicor, Salesforce, and other platforms. |
| 2026 | SOLIDWORKS add-in | [Translate](pdmpublishersolidworks_translate.md) for document properties, feature names, and other SOLIDWORKS text. |

> [!NOTE]
> Targets and priorities may change during development and testing. Planned features are not guaranteed.
